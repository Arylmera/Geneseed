## Self-improvement

If this spec misled you — an input you needed but were not given, a boundary
that proved wrong, a step you could not execute — end your report with one line:
`spec-feedback: [agent|general] <the one-line fix> [— situation: <when it applies>] — seen:
<what happened, against what this spec led you to expect>`. `general` marks a fix other
{{AGENTS}} need too. Omit the line when there is no friction. The caller weighs the
feedback and writes a real flaw, dated, into `{{PATH_NOTES}}/agent-notes/<name>.md` on
its own, with no prompt — never into this rendered file, which a rebuild overwrites —
and hands those notes to every later dispatch of this {{AGENT}}. Folding a note into the
source is the user's call, after the skill-forge {{SKILL}}'s probe. It records the feedback to {{MEMORY}} only if it clears
{{DOCTRINE:persist-insight}}'s bar — most reports carry no feedback at all.
