# {{SKILL}}: quiz

> {{DESC_QUIZ}}

<!-- invocation: user -->

**Trigger:** the user wants what they know *tested*, not explained — "drill me on X",
"quiz me until it sticks", "let me explain X to you and catch me", "I think I already
know X, prove me wrong", "find the holes in what I know". Three shapes, picked from the
ask: a **drill** (practise until reflex), a **feynman** (explain it back, get caught on
the stumbles), or a **gap check** (a mastery claim stress-tested). To be taught instead,
use the [teach {{SKILL}}](teach.md).

## Procedure
1. Pin the concept and set the contract plainly: you will test, not lecture, and you
   will not go easy. Do not explain the concept up front.
2. Pick the shape and run it:
   - **Drill** — drop the user straight into a realistic situation where they would
     have to use the concept and would probably slip. On a mistake, withhold the
     answer; ask the one question that forces them to find where their own reasoning
     broke. Reveal only after two honest attempts. New situation each cycle, until they
     get it right without hesitation.
   - **Feynman** — have them explain the topic as if teaching a ten-year-old.
     Interrupt the moment they (a) use jargon they cannot themselves unpack, (b) skip
     a step in the reasoning, or (c) simplify so far the explanation becomes wrong —
     name which of the three it was each time, and keep them going to the end.
   - **Gap check** — ask five questions that look simple but separate someone who
     has gone deep from someone who skimmed: edge cases, the *why*, the assumption
     under the rule, the case where the standard answer breaks. After each answer say
     plainly what it reveals is still missing; a shallow answer is named as shallow,
     with what depth would have looked like.
3. Close with the honest readout, no flattery: what the stumbles or misses reveal is
   not yet solid, what to restudy, and — for a drill — the variation most likely to
   catch them out next. Offer the [teach {{SKILL}}](teach.md) for the gaps that need
   teaching rather than more testing.

## Done when
- The user worked the situation correctly without being handed the answer (drill),
  explained the topic through with every stumble caught and labelled (feynman), or
  answered all five questions and holds a concrete verdict on the remaining gaps (gap
  check) — and the run ended with a direct readout, never empty praise.
