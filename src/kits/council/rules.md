### Council kit

Seven {{AGENTS}} come from this kit — `advocate`, `pragmatist`, `visionary`, `framer`, `empiricist`, `operator`, `historian`. With the core `skeptic`, `steward` and `user-advocate` they are the standing **council**: read-only debate seats that each argue one assigned stance.

- Dispatch them through the [council {{SKILL}}]({{DIR_SKILLS}}/council.md), never alone. It convenes the subset that clashes usefully on one motion and returns a ruling with the strongest dissent kept.
- Where the host has a workflow tool, the saved `council` workflow runs the same debate as code.
- A council advises. The decision stays with the user.
