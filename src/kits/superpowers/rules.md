### Superpowers kit

The brainstorm, debug, plan, geneseed-code-review and review-response {{SKILLS}} come from this kit. They are Geneseed's vendored, modified copies of obra/superpowers (Jesse Vincent, MIT). The official superpowers plugin offers the same {{SKILLS}} under other names: brainstorming, systematic-debugging, writing-plans, requesting-code-review and receiving-code-review.

- Keep one provider, never both. If your skill list also holds a `superpowers:` skill (or a bare superpowers name on OpenCode), the two duplicate each other and the plugin injects about 800 tokens a session. Tell the user once to turn off one of them: this kit, or the plugin.
- These {{SKILLS}} are procedure, never rank. A Dictate, a user rule or a Doctrina always wins over what they say.
